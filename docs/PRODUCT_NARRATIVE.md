# ROCKSOUL — PRODUCT NARRATIVE

> **MORE THAN AGI. ONE STEP TOWARD ASI.**  
> **THINK WITH CHAOS. ACT WITH RIOT.**  
> **WORK HARD. PLAY HARD.**

## 1. The one-line story

**Rocksoul is a local-first creative-intelligence studio that turns rough ideas and difficult work into scoped missions, useful artifacts, and evidence-backed outcomes—with the human in control.**

Rocksoul is not a chat window with a punk jacket. It is a persistent workbench: it remembers project state, helps explore more than one route, brings in the right models and tools, makes tangible work, checks the result, and keeps the receipts.

**Less prompt-loop. More let's-build-this.**

## 2. The manifesto

Ideas should be allowed to get weird. Execution should not be allowed to get careless.

Rocksoul gives people room to connect disciplines, challenge assumptions, and try unconventional approaches. Then it turns that energy into clear scope, bounded actions, verifiable work, and honest decisions.

The product's attitude is ambitious. Its claims must stay measurable.

- **“More Than AGI”** is a provocation about ambition and useful intelligence—not a claim that the product has achieved or surpassed AGI.
- **“One Step Toward ASI”** is a distant research horizon—not a promise, schedule, guaranteed path, or capability statement.
- Rocksoul does not claim consciousness, sentience, guaranteed correctness, or unrestricted autonomy.

## 3. AGILITY: how Rocksoul behaves

AGILITY is the product's operating philosophy, not a benchmark score or model label.

| Principle | Meaning in product behavior |
|---|---|
| **Adaptive** | Re-plan when evidence, constraints, or user intent changes. Never silently rewrite the mission. |
| **Generative** | Explore alternatives, hypotheses, and cross-discipline connections before converging. |
| **Intelligent** | Use context, tools, and models deliberately; distinguish known facts from guesses. |
| **Grounded** | Attach claims to sources, tool output, tests, and uncertainty. |
| **Integrated** | Connect projects, files, models, tools, artifacts, and people through explicit contracts. |
| **Tactical** | Match the action to risk, budget, permissions, and time; stop when prerequisites are missing. |
| **Human-led** | The user owns goals, permission grants, consequential approvals, and final disposition. |

## 4. Who Rocksoul is for

Primary users are people who move between disciplines and need continuity from thought to finished work:

- **Builders** who move between code, product, design, and research.
- **Developers** who need repository-aware assistance without giving an agent unrestricted access.
- **Creators** who combine text, visual, audio, video, and prototype work.
- **Researchers** who need hypotheses, experiments, sources, and decisions to remain traceable.
- **Founders and operators** who want to test an idea rather than merely pitch it.
- **Small teams** that need a specialist crew with clear ownership—not an unaccountable swarm.

Rocksoul is not initially a companionship chatbot, generic CRM, autonomous employee, public cloud publishing robot, or replacement for human judgment.

## 5. The six-beat creative loop

The six beats are the product's user mental model. They are not required to be six separate screens; the interface may combine them while preserving the underlying contracts.

1. **SPARK — Capture the signal.** Bring an idea, question, repository, file, link, voice note, or problem into a project.
2. **RIFF — Shape the mission.** Define the objective, scope, constraints, sources, acceptance criteria, privacy class, and what must not happen.
3. **THE PIT — Assemble the crew.** Select the smallest useful set of models, specialist roles, tools, and human collaborators. Each gets a bounded assignment.
4. **FORGE — Make the work.** Build a report, plan, prototype, patch, dataset, design, or other tangible artifact through authorized operations.
5. **SOUND CHECK — Challenge the result.** Inspect sources, run checks, compare versions, test acceptance criteria, expose disagreement, and record uncertainty.
6. **STAGE — Decide and hand off.** The human may approve, export, publish through a separately authorized workflow, revise, reject, or archive.

**Exploration may be divergent. Execution must be scoped. Verification must be observable. Completion must be earned.**

## 6. The Rocksoul promise

Rocksoul should reliably provide five things:

1. **Continuity:** project and mission state survive app restarts and are understandable later.
2. **Context with boundaries:** the user can inspect what information is selected and what may leave the device.
3. **Useful action:** tools do concrete work within explicit capabilities and budgets.
4. **Proof:** artifacts carry versions, provenance, test results, approvals, and unresolved questions.
5. **Agency:** the user can stop, correct, revoke, reject, restore, and delete.

The first useful journey is deliberately narrower: create a local project, draft a mission, inspect metadata safely, create an artifact, review evidence, approve it, and reopen the result after restart. The current implementation proves only this first slice; it is not yet a general research or coding agent.

## 7. What makes the studio distinctive

### Chaos-to-Order
Generate multiple hypotheses and routes, record assumptions, run cheap reversible experiments, then converge using evidence and constraints.

### A crew with jobs, not a mystery swarm
Builder, Skeptic, Researcher, Operator, Editor, and Guardian are possible roles. A role has a contract, output schema, context scope, capability grants, budget, and acceptance criteria. Start with one reliable worker and one verifier; expand only when evaluation shows a measurable benefit.

### Memory with provenance
Memory is not an opaque transcript dump. Events, observations, facts, interpretations, procedures, and preferences have distinct types, source, scope, time, confidence, correction history, and deletion behavior.

### Proof-carrying creation
A meaningful artifact can show how it was produced: mission and criteria version, source references, tool actions, environment, checks, approvals, hashes, and remaining uncertainty.

### Hybrid workbench
The long-term work surface may span files, repository tools, browser, desktop UI, APIs, CLI, and creative media. Every adapter must use the same policy, trace, cancellation, and verification model.

## 8. Brand and experience

**Brand character:** riot creative, grunge, Gen Z, funky, DIY studio energy—with engineering discipline beneath the surface.

**Voice:** punchy, alive, clever, direct, and occasionally cheeky. Never vague, mystical, manipulative, or corporate-brochure filler.

**Visual direction already established in the first UI slice:**
- Asphalt #09090b and ink #151519 for the workbench.
- Warm paper #f1ebdd and dust #a7a29a for readable content.
- Riot pink #ff315c, acid lime #d8ff35, electric cyan #38ddf2, and amber #ffb547 as purposeful accents.
- Display typography: Anton/Impact-style condensed headlines.
- Working typography: Space Grotesk; metadata and evidence: Space Mono.
- Hard-edged panels, tape labels, print/halftone-inspired texture, restrained skew/rotation, and energetic but short motion.

Color is not a substitute for status text. Keyboard focus, contrast, reduced motion, responsive layout, and screen-reader semantics are mandatory. The visual system must never hide destructive risk behind decoration.

## 9. Product boundaries

### Default posture
- Local-first persistence.
- Rust owns filesystem, process, database, credentials, permissions, and policy decisions.
- React renders state and requests typed operations; it never authorizes a capability.
- Tools, skills, model outputs, imported files, and network responses are untrusted.
- Network egress, content reads, writes, shell, and computer use are denied until a narrowly scoped capability is granted.
- A consequential action needs a preview, policy decision, explicit approval when required, timeout/cancellation, and evidence.
- Sensitive content and secrets do not enter ordinary logs.
- No fake health, sync, progress, completion, or verification state.

### Explicit non-promises
Rocksoul does not promise that a model is correct, that agents are safe because they follow prompts, that an inventory proves a repository is healthy, or that an experiment predicts the real world. A model's statement is not evidence that a task passed.

## 10. The long horizon

1. **Useful local studio:** dependable project and mission continuity, safe read-only work, artifacts, and evidence.
2. **Reliable mission engine:** context compilation, a real model adapter, plans, cancellable tools, recovery, and verification.
3. **Controlled making:** exact approvals, isolated workspaces, patch review, apply journal, and rollback.
4. **Proof-carrying creation:** portable evidence and independently inspectable deliverables.
5. **Adaptive memory and crew:** typed memory, evaluated retrieval, portable skills, and small specialist teams.
6. **Reality Loom:** controlled browser and desktop interaction through semantic interfaces and observable traces.
7. **Model DJ:** policy-first selection of cloud and local models using measured quality, latency, cost, and availability.
8. **Creative Collider and research lab:** multimodal creation, simulations, and AGILITY research—only after the core remains safe and measurable.

There is no guaranteed ladder from today's AI to AGI or ASI. Rocksoul earns every capability claim one tested vertical slice at a time.
